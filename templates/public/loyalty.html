Questions cafe owners ask.

Do my customers need an app?

No. They scan the QR with their phone camera, WhatsApp opens, and they tap send.

What if a customer forgets to scan?

Staff can add the visit from the Loyalty dashboard using the customer's number.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

No. We connect the WhatsApp Business number you already use, and you keep using the app.

Can I use it with Verzio Book?

Yes. Both work on the same number: bookings as usual, and rewards when customers scan.

What is the pilot?

Verzio Loyalty is new. Our first cafes get the pilot price and a direct line to us while we fine-tune it together.
