Questions

What salon owners ask.

Can clients still call or walk in?+

Yes. Staff add walk-ins from the dashboard, and the diary keeps every booking in one place.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?+

No. We connect the WhatsApp Business number you already use, and your app keeps working.

How do points work for different services?+

Clients earn on what they spend. You choose the rate and the rewards, like a free hair spa at 2,000 points.

Can I have more than one stylist?+

Yes. Set how many clients you can take at the same time, and each booking shows who it's with.

What does WhatsApp charge?+

Meta bills your salon directly for some messages. We help you add ₹500 on setup day, and you see what you've used.
