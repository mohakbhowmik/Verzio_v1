How we count it

Only business Verzio actually caused.

  • New customers who joined through a member's invite
  • Customers who hadn't visited in 30+ days and came back
  • Visits within 7 days of a Verzio offer: birthday, win-back or boost
  • Repeat visits by people who were new to your café when they joined

Each counted visit × the average bill we agree on day one. Regulars who'd have come anyway aren't counted. WhatsApp message charges are paid by your café to Meta and aren't part of the refund.

Questions

What café owners ask.

Do my customers need an app?+

No. Everything happens in WhatsApp, plus a wallet page that opens in their browser.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?+

No. We connect the WhatsApp Business number you already use, and your app keeps working.

What does WhatsApp charge?+

Meta bills your café directly for some messages. We help you add ₹500 on setup day, and you see what you've used in your dashboard.

Does it connect to my POS?+

Not yet. Staff just tap Give when a reward is claimed, so it works alongside any billing system.

What if a customer forgets their phone?+

Staff can add a visit by phone number from the dashboard in a few seconds.
