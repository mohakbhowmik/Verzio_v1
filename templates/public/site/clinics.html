Questions

What doctors ask.

Can patients still call or walk in?+

Yes. Staff add phone bookings and walk-ins from the dashboard, so everything is in one diary.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?+

No. We connect the WhatsApp Business number you already use, and your app keeps working.

What patient data do you keep?+

Only what's needed to book: name, WhatsApp number and the appointment. No medical records.

Can I have more than one doctor?+

Yes. The multi-doctor plan lets you see several patients at the same time.

What does WhatsApp charge?+

Meta bills your clinic directly for reminders, usually a few paise each. We help you add ₹500 on setup day.
