Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Verzio Studio ("Verzio", "we") provides WhatsApp booking software to salons, spas, clinics and similar businesses ("businesses"). This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, and your choices. It applies to business owners who use Verzio and to customers who message a business that uses Verzio.

What we collect

  • Customers of a business: WhatsApp phone number, WhatsApp profile name, the messages you send to that business's WhatsApp number, and your booking details (service, date, time, status).
  • Business owners: name, email, phone numbers, business details, opening hours, services and prices, and the WhatsApp Business account details shared with us when you connect your number.
  • Technical data: server logs needed to run and secure the service.

How we use it

  • To take, confirm, remind, reschedule and cancel bookings on the business's behalf.
  • To pass customer questions to the business and show the business its bookings.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • The business you are booking with, which decides how your booking is handled.
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp), which carries the messages under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Our hosting provider, which stores the data on our behalf.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Roles

For customer data, the business is responsible for deciding why and how it is used; Verzio processes it on the business's instructions to provide the booking service.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept while the business uses Verzio and for up to 12 months after it stops, unless the law requires longer. Backups are kept for up to 14 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access keys for WhatsApp accounts are stored encrypted, and admin access is password-protected.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law. Contact the business you booked with, or us directly at the number below. We will respond within 30 days. Customers can also simply stop messaging the business's number.
