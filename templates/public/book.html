Questions owners ask.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

No. Verzio connects to the WhatsApp Business number you already use. Your customers keep messaging the same number.

Will I still see my chats in the WhatsApp Business app?

Yes. Every conversation, including the bot's replies, stays in your app. If you reply to someone yourself, the bot steps back for that person.

What do my customers need to do?

Nothing new. They send "Hi" to your number and tap through a short menu. No app, no website, no sign-up.

How long does setup take?

About 15 minutes in one visit. You log in with Facebook, confirm on your phone, and we add your services and hours.

Who pays for WhatsApp messages?

They're included in your plan. Replies to customers who message you are free on WhatsApp; reminders and alerts cost a few paise each, and we cover them.

Can I stop any time?

Yes. Plans are monthly. Your WhatsApp number and chats stay yours if you leave.
