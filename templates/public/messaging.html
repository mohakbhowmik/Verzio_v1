Questions owners ask.

Who can I send messages to?

Customers who have agreed to hear from you: people who booked with you, collected loyalty points, or gave you their number for offers. You can upload a list too, as long as everyone on it agreed.

Why do you check each campaign?

WhatsApp limits numbers that send messages people don't want. A quick check protects your business number, which is also the number your customers use to reach you.

What counts towards my 1,000 messages?

Only the offers you send. Booking confirmations, reminders and replies to customers who message you first don't count.

Do I need a new number?

No. Messages go out from the WhatsApp Business number you already use, with your business name.
