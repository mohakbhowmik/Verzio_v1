# Verzio Studio

## Product Vision

Verzio is a WhatsApp-first SaaS platform for appointment-based businesses like salons, clinics and spas.

Customers book appointments through WhatsApp.

Business owners manage bookings primarily through WhatsApp.

The business keeps its existing WhatsApp Business number using Meta Embedded Signup / Coexistence.

Verzio is multi-tenant.

Adding a new business should never require code changes.

---

## MVP Scope

Customer books appointment.

↓

Booking stored in database.

↓

Business owner receives approval request.

↓

Owner confirms/rejects.

↓

Customer receives confirmation.

↓

Morning appointment summary.

↓

Night Excel report.
